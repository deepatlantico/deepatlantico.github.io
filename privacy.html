Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how Deep Atlantico Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by Deep Atlântico Records, Lisbon, Portugal ("we"), handles information. The App is used only by the owner of the Deep Atlântico Records YouTube channel to manage that channel.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App receives an OAuth access token for the owner's own YouTube channel. With it, the App accesses only:

  • basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm that it is working on the correct channel;
  • the channel's playlists, to add new videos to them;
  • the videos the App uploads, and the channel's scheduled videos, to set and check their details (title, description, tags, translations, thumbnail, privacy status and publish time);
  • optionally, posting a comment from our own channel on our own published videos.

The App does not access or collect information about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube users, and it does not read other people's comments, messages or analytics.

3. How information is used

The information is used only to upload, schedule and manage our own videos on our own channel. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage

The OAuth token and a small log of which of our videos were uploaded are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. We do not operate a server and we do not store this information anywhere else.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information obtained through the App to third parties.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the App's folder on the owner's computer deletes all locally stored data. For any request, email deepatlantico@gmail.com.

7. Website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address under Google's own privacy policy.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
