Deep Atlântico Records is an independent music project based in Lisbon, Portugal. We publish original sunset deep house, organic house and bossa house mixes inspired by the Atlantic coast — from the Algarve and Lisbon to Rio de Janeiro — on our YouTube channel @deepatlantico, with a new mix every Saturday.

Deep Atlantico Uploader

Deep Atlantico Uploader is a small private command-line tool that Deep Atlântico Records uses to manage its own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and is used only by the channel owner.

What the app does, using the YouTube Data API:

  • uploads our own finished music videos and Shorts to our channel as private videos with a scheduled publish time;
  • sets each video's title, description, tags, category, language, translations (localizations), custom thumbnail and AI-content disclosure;
  • adds the videos to our own playlists and, if we choose, posts a comment on our own published videos;
  • reads back our channel's scheduled videos so we can check that everything is correct.

The app never publishes a video immediately, never accesses other people's channels and does not collect personal data from viewers or any other users. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
